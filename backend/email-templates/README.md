# Aarambh email templates

This folder holds **copy and markup only**. Nothing here is imported by the backend or connected to an email sender. Adding these files did not send mail, create local outbox messages, or change SMTP configuration. SMTP is not configured in the current environment; existing application delivery behavior was left untouched.

## Template inventory

| Template | Subject line | Backend event and status |
|---|---|---|
| `account-verification` | Please verify your Aarambh email | Account registration and verification resend already use this event; this asset is not wired into the existing sender. |
| `password-reset` | Reset your Aarambh password | Password reset already exists; this asset is not wired into the existing sender. |
| `launch-published-followers` | A new launch you follow is live: `{{launch_title}}` | Launch publication already creates in-app notifications for followers of a founder, business, or category. Email is not enabled for that event. |

Each template has a `.html` body and a `.txt` plain-text fallback. Subjects and preheaders are listed below; the sender must supply them separately if these assets are integrated later.

## Variables

Templates use literal `{{variable_name}}` placeholders. Substitute only when an email-delivery feature is intentionally enabled:

- `{{display_name}}`: account display name; use a neutral greeting such as “there” when unavailable.
- `{{verification_url}}`, `{{reset_url}}`: one-time account-action URLs generated by the backend, never logged or exposed in analytics.
- `{{expires_in_minutes}}`: expiry duration for a one-time link.
- `{{launch_title}}`, `{{business_name}}`: published launch and business names.
- `{{launch_url}}`: public launch URL. `{{manage_launch_url}}`: authenticated founder-workspace URL.
- `{{launch_time}}`, `{{end_time}}`: display-ready times in Asia/Kolkata.
- `{{content_type}}`, `{{content_name}}`, `{{decision_summary}}`, `{{reason}}`: moderator-reviewed values. Render supplied reason text as untrusted text.
- `{{leaderboard_status}}`: concise, recipient-friendly wording such as “placed on hold” or “reinstated”; do not pass an internal enum through unchanged.
- `{{appeal_status}}`: concise wording for accepted/restored, dismissed, or remaining under review; do not pass an internal enum through unchanged.
- `{{moderation_url}}`: authenticated Aarambh page for reviewing a decision or appeal.
- `{{app_url}}`: Aarambh home URL.

All placeholders are examples, not real user data. Before rendering, HTML-escape text values; validate/allowlist URL schemes and hosts before inserting links; escape attribute values separately; do not render user- or moderator-supplied text as HTML. Do not add account emails, credentials, access tokens, or other secrets to templates.

## Scope notes

- No sender, SMTP transport, runtime configuration, route, scheduler, or frontend behavior was changed.
- Launch, follow, and reminder messages are **prepared for future use only**; they currently remain in-app notifications. No email cadence, preference, or unsubscribe behavior is established for these events, so do not enable them until those product choices are made.
- Security, moderation, and appeal copy is transactional. Keep one-time links short-lived and single-use, and keep reasons and content names limited to what the recipient needs to act.
- The account-verification message confirms an account email only; it must not be described as an Aarambh business-verification badge.
